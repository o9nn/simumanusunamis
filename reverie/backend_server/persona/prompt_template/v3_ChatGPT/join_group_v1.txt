PART 1 - About the Persona:

Task: !<INPUT 1>! notices a group of people nearby. Should they join?

Consider:
- How well does !<INPUT 1>! know the group members?
- Is what they're doing interesting to !<INPUT 1>!?
- Does !<INPUT 1>! have time? Are they busy with something important?
- What's !<INPUT 1>!'s current mood - do they feel social?
- Would joining be natural or awkward?

{

"confidence": "<high, medium, or low confidence in this decision>",
"reason": "<brief explanation of why to join or not join>",
"approach_style": "<if joining: 'wave and walk over', 'wait for invitation', 'casual greeting', etc.>",
