A group conversation is taking place between:
!<INPUT 0>!

Group context: !<INPUT 3>!

Current topic being discussed: !<INPUT 2>!

!<INPUT 1>!

---
Task: Analyze the conversation and determine:
1. Whether the current topic has been sufficiently explored
2. Whether the conversation should naturally shift to a new topic
3. What natural topic transitions might occur

Consider:
- Has the current topic been discussed from multiple angles?
- Are participants showing signs of wanting to move on (brief responses, changing subject)?
- Is there a natural segue to a related topic?
- Has someone introduced something that could become the new focus?

Output format: Output a json of the following format:
{
"current_topic_exhausted": <true or false>,
"suggested_new_topic": "<a natural new topic to discuss, or null if continuing current topic>",
"transition_trigger": "<what in the conversation suggests a topic change, or 'none'>",
"natural_transition_phrase": "<a phrase someone could use to shift topics naturally>",
"conversation_energy": "<high, medium, or low - based on engagement level>"
}
